### Project Description

This is a scrollytelling interactive data story that follows the journey of a fictional
clinical trial — from the moment it opens enrollment to the moment it closes — revealing
at each stage who drops out and why.

The central thesis is provocative and human: **clinical trials don't fail because of bad
science. They fail because the system was never designed for the people it needs most.**

The story moves through a patient enrollment funnel, surfacing how geography,
transportation, digital access, work schedules, language, and insurance status quietly
eliminate candidates before a single dose is administered. It ends with a reframe: what
would enrollment look like if access were designed in from the start?

### Who This Is For

The audience is anyone who works in or adjacent to healthcare — designers, strategists,
researchers, and executives — but it should be emotionally accessible to anyone who has
ever been a patient.
